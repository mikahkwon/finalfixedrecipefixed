Egg Rice With Soy Sauce

My go-to when I'm hungry and lazy.

My Recipe

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Rice 1 bowl
Egg 1
Soy sauce 1 spoon
Sesame oil 1 spoon
Seaweed A little
Butter A small piece

Steps

  1. Fry the egg. Keep the yolk runny.
  2. Put the egg on top of the hot rice.
  3. Add soy sauce and sesame oil.
  4. Crush seaweed on top.
  5. Add butter.
  6. Mix and eat.
